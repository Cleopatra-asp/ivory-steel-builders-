01 — Who We Are

A steel-first alternative to conventional construction

Ivory Steel Builders (ISB) specializes in the design, detailing, fabrication, erection, sheeting and civil works of Pre‑Engineered Building Systems (PEB) on a full turnkey basis. Launched in 2021, ISB set out to change how Bangladesh thinks about steel structures — delivering cost‑effective, quality projects within the schedules our customers agree to.

Steel components can be reused and recycled, cutting raw‑material waste and what would otherwise reach incineration or landfill — a real consideration in one of the world's most densely populated nations.
